Tenant improvements · Colorado rules

Short answer: in Colorado, public and commercial buildings need an inspection by a state-certified asbestos building inspector before more than 160 square feet of material, or 260 linear feet of pipe, is disturbed. Painting over intact walls does not usually disturb material. Sanding, scraping, removing texture and demolition can.

Why this comes up on repaint jobs

A straight repaint of sound walls is a coating going on, not material coming off. Tenant improvement work is different. New layouts mean walls come down, ceilings get opened, textured surfaces get skimmed or scraped, and old finishes get sanded. In older buildings, some of those materials can contain asbestos, and Colorado regulates their disturbance before the work starts, not after.

The thresholds, side by side

Building typeInspection required before disturbing more than
Public and commercial buildings160 sq ft of material, or 260 linear ft of pipe
Single-family residences32 sq ft of surfaces, 50 linear ft of pipe, or a 55-gallon drum volume

CDPHE's guidance also describes a possible exemption for buildings constructed after October 12, 1988. Check the guidance directly for how that applies to your building.

How it fits a TI schedule

  1. Before demo: the owner or GC arranges the inspection of materials the work will disturb.
  2. Results in hand: if materials test positive, abatement by licensed professionals happens before other trades proceed in that area.
  3. Painting: prep that sands, scrapes or removes texture waits for clearance in that area.
What we do: we ask whether an inspection has been done on any TI bid that involves removing texture or old finishes, and we do not sand or scrape suspect material until results are in. We are painters, not an abatement contractor, and we say so in the bid.

Voice answer: does painting over asbestos need a permit in Colorado?

Painting over intact material is generally not a disturbance. The CDPHE inspection rules apply when material will be disturbed beyond the thresholds. If you are unsure, ask CDPHE or a certified inspector before work starts.

Questions we get

Do I need an asbestos inspection before repainting a commercial building in Colorado?

Not for painting over intact surfaces. An inspection by a Colorado-certified asbestos building inspector is required before disturbing more than 160 square feet of material, or 260 linear feet of pipe, in public and commercial buildings, per CDPHE.

What is the trigger for a single-family home?

For single-family homes, CDPHE's trigger is lower: more than 32 square feet of surfaces, 50 linear feet of pipe, or a 55-gallon drum's volume of material. Buildings constructed after October 12, 1988 may qualify for an exemption.

Who arranges the asbestos inspection on a TI project?

Usually the building owner or the general contractor, before demolition or surface removal begins. The painter needs the results before sanding, scraping or removing texture.
